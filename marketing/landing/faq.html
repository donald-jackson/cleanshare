FAQ

Quick answers to common questions.

Why doesn't WhatsApp or Instagram show the cleaned metadata?

Apps like WhatsApp and Instagram re-encode images and videos when you upload them, which already discards most metadata on their side. The problem is everything that happens before that — files you AirDrop, email, save to cloud storage, or send through apps that don't re-encode all carry your original EXIF, GPS, and camera data. CleanShare strips that metadata before the file ever leaves your device, so it's gone no matter where the photo ends up.

Can I batch-clean multiple photos at once?

Yes. Open CleanShare and pick as many photos or videos as you like through the system photo picker (PHPicker), and it cleans them all in one pass before handing them to the share sheet.

Does it work on iPad?

Yes. CleanShare is a universal app that runs on both iPhone and iPad, iOS/iPadOS 17.0 and later.
