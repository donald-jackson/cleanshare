About

CleanShare is a free and open-source iOS app that strips identifying metadata — EXIF, GPS coordinates, camera make and model, serial numbers, timestamps, MakerNote blocks, and XMP — from photos and videos before you share them. It lives in the iOS share sheet, processes everything on-device with zero network calls, and re-presents the system share sheet with cleaned copies ready for any destination. There are no accounts, no analytics, and no third-party SDKs; the privacy guarantees are enforced mechanically by CI on every commit. CleanShare is MIT licensed and fully auditable.

One-liners

  • CleanShare strips the hidden metadata from your photos and videos before you share them — right from the iOS share sheet.
  • No servers, no accounts, no tracking: everything happens on your device, and CI proves it on every commit.
  • Free, open source, and MIT licensed — privacy you can read line by line.
