Support

CleanShare has no accounts and collects no data — most questions are answered below.

Report a bug or request a feature

The fastest way to reach the maintainers is a GitHub issue. Please include your device model, iOS version, and the file type you were cleaning.

Open an issue on GitHub

Found a case where metadata survived cleaning? Use the "Privacy leak report" issue template — those are treated as the highest priority.

Not on GitHub? Email apple@ddj.solutions and we'll help.

Common questions

  • Where did my cleaned photo go? After cleaning, CleanShare opens the system share sheet so you can send the cleaned copy anywhere. Your original is never modified.
  • Does it work on iPad? Yes — CleanShare is a universal app for iPhone and iPad on iOS/iPadOS 17.0 and later.
  • Why does the destination app still show a date? See the FAQ — apps often add their own timestamps after you share; CleanShare removes the metadata embedded in the file itself.

Privacy

CleanShare works entirely on your device, makes no network connections, and collects no data. Read the full privacy policy, or audit the source yourself at github.com/donald-jackson/cleanshare.
