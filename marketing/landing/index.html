CleanShare

Strip the hidden metadata from your photos and videos before you share them.

Free. Open source. On-device. No tracking, ever.

Coming soon App Store Beta TestFlight

What gets stripped

GPS coordinates, camera make and model, serial numbers, timestamps, MakerNote blocks, and XMP — every identifying tag EXIF and friends can carry, gone before the file ever leaves your device.

How it works

CleanShare lives in the iOS share sheet. Pick your photos, share to CleanShare, and it re-presents the system share sheet with cleaned copies — ready for WhatsApp, Messages, Instagram, or anywhere else.

Privacy by design

Everything happens on your iPhone or iPad. No servers, no accounts, no analytics, no network calls — verified by CI. The whole app is MIT licensed and auditable.
