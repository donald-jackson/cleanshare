# About the maintainer

<maintainer name> is the creator and maintainer of CleanShare, a free and open-source iOS app that strips identifying metadata from photos and videos before they're shared. CleanShare grew out of a simple frustration: ordinary sharing flows leak GPS coordinates, camera models, and timestamps to every recipient, and nothing on the phone removes them. The project is built entirely on Apple's own frameworks with zero third-party code, and its no-network, no-tracking guarantees are enforced by CI on every commit.
