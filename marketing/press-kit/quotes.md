# Maintainer quotes

## Quote 1 — the "why"

> Every photo you send carries your location, your camera, and the exact moment you took it. People deserve to share a picture without handing over all of that, and CleanShare makes that the default instead of an afterthought.

## Quote 2 — the "how"

> CleanShare lives in the share sheet, so cleaning a photo is just one extra tap. It strips the hidden metadata, leaves your originals untouched, and hands you back a clean copy ready for any app.

## Quote 3 — the "what's different"

> There's no account, no analytics, and no network access at all — and that's not a promise, it's checked by CI on every commit. The whole thing is MIT-licensed, so you don't have to take my word for it.
