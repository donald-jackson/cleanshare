## v0.1.0 — Initial release

Welcome to CleanShare — strip the hidden metadata from your photos and videos before you share them. Everything happens on your device; nothing is uploaded, and no account is required.

- Share-sheet integration: scrub photos and videos straight from any app's share sheet, then hand off to your chosen destination.
- In-app cleaning: pick photos and videos with the system picker and clean them in a tap.
- Try it on a sample photo: see exactly which EXIF, GPS, and camera fields get removed before you trust it with your own files.
- Live Photos, your way: choose to keep the motion, flatten to a still, or strip metadata from both halves.
- Broad format support: HEIC/HEIF, JPEG, PNG, and common video formats with lossless passthrough.
- Settings you control: keep or remove orientation, ICC color profile, and capture date — GPS is always removed.
- Zero data collected: no analytics, no trackers, no servers. Open source and auditable.
