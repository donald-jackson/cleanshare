# App Store promotional text

> Apple lets you update promo text WITHOUT a binary submission; use it for time-sensitive announcements (press pickups, sales, news).

## v1.0 launch

The chosen promotional text (≤170 chars), per PLAN.md §13.1:

> "Share photos and videos without leaking your location, camera model, or timestamps. One tap, no accounts, nothing leaves your device."

(133 chars)

## Variants

Swap any of these into App Store Connect without a new build. All ≤170 chars.

**A — feature focus** (146 chars)

> "Strip GPS, camera make/model, timestamps and EXIF from photos and videos before you share. Works in the share sheet. Free, open source, on-device."

**B — emotional** (136 chars)

> "Your photos remember where you were. CleanShare doesn't. Erase the hidden metadata before you send — one tap, nothing leaves your phone."

**C — credibility** (150 chars)

> "Open-source metadata scrubber for iPhone and iPad. No accounts, no servers, no tracking — privacy you can audit. Removes EXIF, GPS and MakerNote data."
